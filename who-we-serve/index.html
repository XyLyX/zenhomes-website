Six Client Profiles, One Advisory Standard

A deliberately concentrated practice — quality over quantity. Here's who we work with, and what the engagement actually looks like for each.

Home / Six Client Profiles, One Advisory Standard

By Navvin S Bhalla · Zen Homes Advisory — Last updated August 28, 2026

Zen Homes doesn't try to be the right fit for every buyer in the Dubai market. The practice is built around a small number of client profiles whose needs share a common thread: property that has to perform as part of a wider capital strategy, not just close as a single transaction. What follows is who those clients are, and what working with an advisor rather than a broker actually looks like for each of them.

Luxury Residences

Owners and prospective owners of luxury apartments and villas across Dubai's premier addresses — Palm Jumeirah, Emirates Hills, Downtown Dubai, Bluewaters Island, Jumeirah Bay Island. For this client, the engagement typically centers on sourcing the right unit ahead of public listing, verifying developer and title standing before any payment moves, and — for owners already holding a residence — advising on when a market cycle favors holding versus selling. See our ultra-prime market data guide for how we read this segment.

Portfolio Investors

Individuals and entities building or rebalancing a multi-asset property portfolio across more than one Dubai community, and in some cases across more than one emirate. The work here is less about any single acquisition and more about portfolio-level questions: service charge exposure across holdings, when a rebalance out of an oversupplied community makes sense, and how a new acquisition fits alongside what's already owned. Our portfolio management guide covers the specific mechanics — service charges, fees, and governance — this client profile deals with most.

International Buyers

Overseas principals acquiring UAE real estate as part of a global asset allocation, often without the ability to visit Dubai between key decision points. The engagement leans heavily on remote-transaction mechanics — power of attorney structures, escrow verification, and developer or resale due diligence conducted on the client's behalf — alongside the same portfolio-fit questions any allocator asks before adding a new asset class or geography.

Family Offices

Multi-generational wealth structures seeking durable, well-governed property holdings rather than opportunistic, cycle-timed purchases. Family office engagements typically run longest and touch the widest range of the practice — acquisition, ongoing portfolio oversight, succession-relevant structuring questions, and periodic market intelligence briefings — reflecting the multi-decade horizon this client profile is actually planning around.

NRI Investors

Non-resident Indian and India-resident investors seeking a trusted, on-the-ground advisory presence in Dubai — a profile with its own distinct set of questions around FEMA/LRS remittance rules, DTAA and Indian tax treatment, and Golden Visa eligibility that a generalist broker typically can't answer with any precision. This is a large enough client base that we maintain a dedicated research practice for it: the full India & NRI investor guide covers legal eligibility, money transfer mechanics, tax treatment, Golden Visa thresholds, due diligence, and rental yield comparisons against Indian cities, all data-sourced rather than broker-marketed.

High-Net-Worth Individuals

Individuals and families whose net worth calls for a bespoke, discretion-first advisory relationship — where privacy in how an acquisition is sourced and structured matters as much as the acquisition itself. This profile often overlaps with the other five above; what distinguishes it is less the transaction size and more the expectation of a single, consistent point of contact who understands the fuller picture of a client's holdings, not just the property in front of them.

How We Work With Each Client Type

Regardless of profile, the engagement follows the same shape: an initial conversation to establish what you're actually trying to accomplish (yield, appreciation, residency, diversification, or some combination), independent research and, where relevant, access to off-market inventory before a recommendation is made, and an ongoing relationship rather than a closed file once a transaction completes. Read more about the advisory model itself, and how it differs structurally from brokerage, on our About page.

Not sure which of these profiles fits your situation? Most clients don't fit neatly into one — tell us what you're trying to accomplish and we'll tell you honestly how we can help.

Frequently Asked

Does Zen Homes work with first-time Dubai property buyers?

Yes, provided the engagement fits the advisory model rather than a single transactional purchase — most first-time buyers who work with us are entering as part of a broader strategy (a first step into portfolio building, a Golden Visa-driven purchase, or an international allocation decision) rather than a one-off purchase with no further relationship expected.

Is there a minimum investment size to work with Zen Homes?

There's no fixed public minimum, but the advisory model is built around ongoing relationships rather than one-off low-value transactions, which naturally shapes the client base toward the profiles described on this page. The honest answer for your specific situation is best had directly — reach out and we'll tell you plainly whether it's a fit.

Do NRI and international clients need to visit Dubai in person?

No. Many international and NRI clients complete acquisitions entirely remotely, using power of attorney structures and advisor-led due diligence in place of in-person visits. See our India & NRI investor guide for the specific mechanics this involves.
