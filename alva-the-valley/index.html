The Design

A deliberate move away from compact townhouse layouts: generous internal dimensions, large floor-to-ceiling windows, and a private outdoor garden plot per home, across two G+1 (ground-plus-one) configurations.

3-Bedroom (~2,788 sq ft). Ground floor is fully open-concept — living and dining flow into an integrated kitchen, alongside a maid's room with en-suite, a laundry station, a powder room, and a rear terrace onto the private garden. All three bedrooms sit on the first floor, separating living from sleeping areas; the primary bedroom has an en-suite bathroom, a walk-in wardrobe, and a private glass-paneled balcony.

4-Bedroom (~3,081 sq ft). A wider built-up footprint, with a ground floor adapted to include a flexible guest bedroom or home office alongside larger multi-car parking bays.

The Location

Alva sits within The Valley, Emaar's community at the Dubai–Al Ain Road (E66) / E77 intersection — roughly 20 minutes from Downtown Dubai and 25 minutes from the airport. The neighbourhood is planned as a low-density eco-community with separated circulation — dedicated cycling tracks and pedestrian trails kept apart from vehicle lanes. It centres on a Town Centre of cafés and boutique retail, with access to Golden Beach, a desert-inspired coastal lagoon with white sand and multi-tier swimming decks, a Sports Village, the Kids' Dale park network, local community retail, and multi-purpose event lawns.

Payment Plan

Market claimEmaar's published terms for Alva: 20% x 5 — five equal annual instalments, running 2026 through 2030, aligned to the Q1 2030 handover. Read as an 80/20 split, that's roughly 80% paid across four instalments during construction (2026–2029) and the final 20% due on completion. A separate monthly option — 2.5% per month, for UAE Nationals and Residents, subject to approval — is also being offered on this release.

Payment plans on off-plan developments are set by the developer and can change before a unit is reserved — treat the above as directional, and confirm the exact schedule and eligibility for the monthly option with your advisor before committing.

Why We're Bringing This to Clients Now

The Valley's prior townhouse release opened in September 2025 and sold into strong demand within months. Alva repeats the same community and the same Emaar terms, but on a deliberately limited release — around 196 homes — with materially bigger plots, built-up areas, and private garden space than that sold-out release, at the quieter E66/E77 corner of the masterplan. We think that combination — proven demand upstream, more land and light per home, on unchanged terms — is worth a client's attention while the release is fresh and still in its planning stage.

Is This the Right Fit?

Alva suits a buyer who wants a standard freehold townhouse in an Emaar master-community with a demonstrated sales track record next door, and who values plot size, natural light, and private outdoor space over being first to a brand-new, unproven location. It is an individually owned, individually titled townhouse purchase — not a fund, leasing structure, or guaranteed-return product. It will not suit a buyer who needs a shorter runway to handover; at a Q1 2030 date, this is a longer hold than some of our other releases.

Frequently Asked

Is Alva individually owned, like a standard Dubai townhouse purchase?

Yes. Alva is a standard freehold off-plan townhouse release within Emaar's The Valley masterplan — each unit is individually owned and individually titled, available to buyers of all nationalities, subject to project terms.

What is the payment plan for Alva?

Emaar's published terms are 20% x 5 — five equal annual instalments from 2026 to 2030, effectively an 80% construction-linked / 20% completion split. UAE Nationals and Residents may also qualify for a 2.5%-per-month alternative, subject to approval. Confirm eligibility and the full schedule with your advisor before reserving.

What extra terms do Zen Homes clients get on this project?

Zen Homes clients transacting on Alva receive additional terms secured through our advisory relationship with Emaar. These are finalized privately at the time of signing rather than advertised publicly — speak with your advisor for specifics.

When does Alva hand over?

Q1 2030. Construction has not yet started as of early 2026 (first land trace was recorded that January) — we'll update this page as milestones are confirmed.
