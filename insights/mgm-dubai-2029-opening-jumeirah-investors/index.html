MGM Dubai Now Opens in Early 2029. What It Means for Jumeirah Investors.

MGM Dubai's 1,500-room island resort now opens in early 2029. What is confirmed, what is not, and what international investors should check.

Home / Insights / MGM Dubai opens early 2029

By Navvin S Bhalla · Zen Homes Advisory — Last updated November 19, 2026

Share Copied

MGM Dubai, the three-brand island resort beside Burj Al Arab, has completed its structure and now targets an early 2029 opening, later than earlier guidance given to investors. Nothing published yet shows an effect on nearby property values, so what follows separates what is confirmed from what is not, and what an international buyer might sensibly check.

What Is Confirmed

  • DataTopping out: developer Wasl Group announced the structural topping out on 16 November 2026. The resort sits on a 3 million sq ft artificial island off Umm Suqeim and the Jumeirah coastline (Zawya, Khaleej Times).
  • DataScope: MGM, Bellagio and ARIA brands with a combined 1,500 rooms, about 40 dining venues, a beach club and a theatre, built by China State Construction Engineering Corporation (Zawya).
  • DataNew date: MGM Resorts' chief content, hospitality and development officer, Ari Kastrati, said MGM is committed to an early 2029 opening. Skift reports the announcement did not say why the date moved.
  • DataEarlier dates: third quarter 2027 was given in April 2025, and the second half of 2028 in July 2025. Skift reports a March 2028 target given to investors in February 2026, while Dubai outlets cite the third quarter of 2028 as the previous date. The sequence is not clean, and depending on the baseline the move is roughly six months to a year.

What Topping Out Does and Does Not Mean

Topping out means the building has reached full height. Interior fit-out, mechanical and electrical systems, testing and commissioning, and the hiring and training of the hotel workforce all follow. Wasl and Gulf outlets describe the project as on track to the new date. Skift counts this as the second delay in about 18 months. The fit-out stage is where large hotel projects most often move again, which is a reason to treat early 2029 as an estimate, not a fixed date.

What We Cannot Say

Market claimWe have found no DLD data or independent analytics linking the delay to prices, rents or yields in Jumeirah or Umm Suqeim. Commentary that the resort will lift nearby values, or that the delay will weigh on them, is opinion rather than evidence. The resort remains a long-term anchor for the coastline. What has changed is the certainty of its timing.

Practical Points for International and NRI Investors

  1. Treat early 2029 as an estimate. Do not underwrite a purchase or a rental assumption on the resort opening on a fixed date.
  2. Separate the catalyst from the asset. A nearby unit should stand on its own pricing, yield and service charges, not on a neighbouring attraction.
  3. Check your own handover terms. If you hold or are considering off-plan units elsewhere, review the handover clauses and the project's payment-protection arrangements with a licensed adviser.
  4. Revisit when a quarter is named. MGM's next earnings materials are the first place a specific opening quarter and updated cost would appear.

What Would Change This View

A stated opening quarter later than mid-2029, a change to the resort's scope or brand mix, or a decision on a gaming licence would each change the picture. So would published transaction data showing a measurable shift in nearby pricing.

Frequently Asked

When does MGM Dubai open?

Early 2029, according to MGM Resorts and developer Wasl Group, announced on 16 November 2026 alongside the structural topping out. Earlier guidance pointed to the third quarter of 2027 and later to 2028. No reason for the latest change was given.

Is the MGM Dubai resort still on track?

Wasl describes it as on track to the new date. Topping out means the structure has reached full height, but fit-out, building systems, testing and commissioning still follow, and that phase is where large hotel projects most often move again.

Does the MGM Dubai delay affect Jumeirah property prices?

We have found no published DLD or independent data linking the delay to prices, rents or yields in Jumeirah or Umm Suqeim. Claims in either direction are opinion rather than evidence.
