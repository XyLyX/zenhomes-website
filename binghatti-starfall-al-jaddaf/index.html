Starfall is Binghatti's newest tower in Al Jaddaf, joining a cluster the developer has already built out along the Dubai Creek corridor. It is genuinely pre-launch: Binghatti's own website carries only a "coming soon" teaser image and a registration form — no official price list, payment plan or floor plans. Three independent Dubai brokers are already circulating consistent pricing, a unit mix and floor plans ahead of a tentative 22 September 2026 launch. This page separates what Binghatti has confirmed from what brokers are reporting, and we will update it as the official launch pack arrives.

Amenities

Market claimStarfall carries an astronomy theme across its architecture, lighting and shared spaces, per broker material: an Observatory deck with telescopes for stargazing, a "Meteor Trail" lighting motif running from the Observatory through the building and grounds, and a double-height lobby with a fibre-optic constellation ceiling, warm bronze detailing and pale stone finishes. Planned facilities include separate adults' and children's pools, a children's play area, a sun lounge, and a yoga and aerobics platform. None of this is confirmed on Binghatti's own site yet.

Why We're Bringing This to Clients Now

Pre-launch is when unit choice and first-release pricing are widest, and Starfall's tentative launch is 22 September — today. The location is unusual in Dubai: metro access now, a Blue Line interchange in 2029, and a high-speed rail station at an unconfirmed future date converge at one node — a rail case that stands on its own without needing the flying-taxi link some marketing implies, which we could not verify. Binghatti also already has a cluster of towers in Al Jaddaf — brokers list Starlight, Ivory, Ghost, Twilight, Moonlight and Wraith — so buyers comparing Starfall have real sibling-building context rather than a blank slate.

Is This the Right Fit?

Starfall suits an investor who wants a lower-ticket, transit-led entry into a central Dubai community and is comfortable acting on broker-sourced pricing ahead of Binghatti's own launch pack. It is less suited to a buyer who needs a confirmed price list, payment schedule and handover date in writing today — on the evidence we could find, none of the three exists yet. Off-plan purchases are individually titled property, not a fund or guaranteed-return product.

What to verify before reserving

Confirmed price list, booking amount and payment schedule in writing; the handover date and delay clause; escrow account and project registration details; estimated service charge per sq ft; unit plans and floor position; whether Binghatti's own master plan shows any rail-linked or air-mobility facility and its approval status; and transfer fees on top of the price.

Frequently Asked

What does Binghatti Starfall cost?

Broker listings consistently show studios from AED 759,999 (average 411 sq ft, smallest unit 348 sq ft), one-bedrooms from AED 1,234,999, and two-bedrooms from AED 1,974,999, with Royal Suite premiums above each. Binghatti has not published an official price list, so treat these as broker guidance pending developer confirmation.

What is the payment plan for Binghatti Starfall?

Three independent broker listings agree on a 50/50 plan for studios, two-bedrooms and retail, and a 60/40 plan for one-bedrooms. Binghatti has not published this directly, so confirm the signed schedule before reserving.

When does Binghatti Starfall hand over?

Broker sources disagree: some list Q4 2027, others 30 September 2028, and one broker's own page states both dates in different sections. Treat the handover date as unconfirmed until Binghatti publishes it.

Is there an Etihad Rail station at Al Jaddaf?

The RTA has named Al Jaddaf as one of two Dubai stations on the planned Abu Dhabi–Dubai high-speed line, with no confirmed opening date. That is separate from Etihad Rail's first Dubai passenger station, opening 30 September 2026 at Al Yalayis in Jumeirah Golf Estates — not Al Jaddaf.

Is there a flying taxi station planned at Binghatti Starfall?

We could not verify one. Joby's four confirmed Dubai vertiports are at Dubai International Airport, Dubai Mall, Atlantis The Royal on Palm Jumeirah, and the American University of Dubai — Al Jaddaf is not among them, and no source ties a vertiport to Starfall's master plan.
