The Location

Nawayef West sits on the hillside portion of Modon's Hudayriyat Island masterplan, elevated toward the Abu Dhabi skyline and Arabian Gulf. It's the same island that recorded AED 19 billion in residential sales in H1 2026 — 27% of every dirham of residential property sold across Abu Dhabi that half-year, more than Saadiyat, Reem and Al Maryah, and Yas Islands combined. Data Bayut's independent price index (May 2026) puts Nawayef West villas at AED 2,018 per sq ft, up 6.07% year-on-year — currently the only Hudayriyat sub-community it tracks in positive territory.

Why We're Bringing This to Clients Now

Most off-plan pitches call themselves "early." This one actually is: five of six layout types have no public price yet. That cuts both ways — there's less to underwrite today, and no guarantee later pricing lands where the market implies. What we can point to is direction, not a promise: Nawayef West is the one Hudayriyat sub-community currently appreciating on Bayut's independent index, while the two closest comparables are both down over the same period. We think that's worth a client's attention before the rest of the range is priced, not a reason to expect a specific number.

Is This the Right Fit?

Nawayef West B suits a buyer comfortable registering ahead of full pricing transparency, in exchange for a genuine first-look position in the sub-community Modon's own hillside data currently favours. It is a standard freehold villa purchase — individually owned and individually titled — not a fund, leasing structure, or guaranteed-return product. It will not suit a buyer who needs complete unit-level pricing and payment terms before making a decision; for that, our Nawayef East B or Bashayer releases are further along.

Frequently Asked

Is Nawayef West B individually owned, like a standard Abu Dhabi villa purchase?

Yes. Nawayef West B is a standard freehold off-plan villa release within Modon's Hudayriyat Island investment zone — each unit is individually owned and individually titled, available to buyers of all nationalities, subject to project and investment-zone terms.

What is the payment plan for Nawayef West B?

Modon has not yet published a payment schedule specific to the West B release. The comparable Nawayef West Homes product currently uses a 40/60 structure (10% on booking, remainder during construction and at handover). Confirm the live West B schedule with your advisor before reserving.

What extra terms do Zen Homes clients get on this project?

Zen Homes clients transacting on Nawayef West B receive additional terms secured through our advisory relationship with Modon. These are finalized privately at the time of signing rather than advertised publicly — speak with your advisor for specifics.

When does Nawayef West B hand over?

Q1 2028, targeted for 30 March 2028, per the developer's current construction timeline. Construction on the wider Nawayef West masterplan began in February 2026.
