Six buildings, one master plan, and six different price points. Ajwan Khorfakkan is Shurooq's beachfront community on Sharjah's East Coast — 185 apartments and duplexes facing the Gulf of Oman with the Hajar Mountains behind — but the developer prices each building on its own terms, and the spread between them is the first thing worth understanding before choosing a unit. This page is the building-by-building listing; for the market case and the developer background, see our Insights briefing.

Building Profiles

Ghadeer — named for the Arabic word for "stream," 24 apartments across five floors with private terraces on every unit and direct boardwalk and marina access. Views run garden, port, island, and partial sea/beach; the entry point into the collection.

Saahil — "shore" in Arabic, the tallest of the three at eight floors and 39 units, with direct boardwalk access and full-privacy positioning on three sides. Garden, beach, sea, port and island views across the collection.

Mawj — 27 apartments across five floors with direct access to Ajwan's private beach — the only one of the three with that distinction. Beach, sea and garden views, described by the developer in a coastal-contemporary design language.

All three sit within 3 minutes of the Al Rabi hiking trail, 5 minutes of the Khorfakkan Amphitheatre and waterfall, 8 minutes of Al Rafisah Dam, and roughly 60 minutes of Sharjah Airport.

The Community

The master plan is anchored around a private marina, dedicated beach access, a waterpark, swimming pools, landscaped grounds, retail and F&B, and a planned hotel. Phase 3's Layan tower fronts the seafront boardwalk and retail area; Juman fronts the marina.

Frequently Asked

Which Ajwan Khorfakkan building should I look at first?

Depends on the mandate: Ghadeer is the lowest entry point with partial views; Saahil and Mawj carry a beachfront/boardwalk premium of roughly 40-50% on comparable duplex stock but offer direct beach or full-privacy positioning.

Who is developing Ajwan Khorfakkan?

The Sharjah Investment and Development Authority (Shurooq), with Darwish Engineering Emirates as main contractor across all phases. See our Insights briefing for the developer's wider portfolio and track record.

When is handover for Ajwan Khorfakkan?

Currently estimated for Q4 2026, per developer/broker listing data. Confirm the current projected date for your specific unit and building with your advisor before reserving.

What is the payment plan for Ajwan Khorfakkan?

A 30/70 structure: 10% on booking, 20% linked to construction milestones, and 70% on handover. Confirm current terms directly with Shurooq's sales office before reserving.

Can international and NRI investors buy at Ajwan Khorfakkan?

Yes. Freehold ownership is open to both UAE/GCC nationals and international investors. Zen Homes advises international and NRI buyers on structuring, remittance and eligibility.
